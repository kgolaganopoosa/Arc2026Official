Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Updated: November 21, 2026 (v1.0.2.100)

1. Data Collection & Zero Server Storage

Arcknowledge — One Stop Internet (Earth 2.0 - Worldly) is built as a client-side web directory and world builder. We do not operate backend user databases, we do not require account registration, and we do not collect, transmit, or store personal data, search queries, or your "My World" places on any Arcknowledge server.

2. Local Browser Storage (My World & Favourites)

Your customized Earth 2.0 - Worldly places, nested malls, notes, and saved favourites are stored locally on your device using your browser's localStorage (arcknowledge_world_v102100 and arcknowledge_favs_v102100). This data never leaves your browser unless you manually export a JSON backup file.

3. Third-Party Links & External Websites

This directory contains outbound links to over 900 independent third-party websites, institutions, banks, retailers, and public services. When you click an external link, you leave Arcknowledge and become subject to that destination site's privacy practices and policies.

4. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA & POPIA)

Because Arcknowledge does not collect or store personal profiles on servers, you retain complete local control over your data and can inspect, export, or erase your My World and Favourites entries at any time directly inside the app or by clearing your browser storage.
