Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026 • Platform: Arcknowledge — One Stop Internet

1. Zero Server-Side Data Collection

Arcknowledge ("we," "our," or "us") operates strictly as a client-side static web directory. We do not operate user accounts, login systems, server-side databases, or remote analytics telemetry. Any search queries, category filters, or tag selections you perform are processed exclusively in memory within your local web browser and are never transmitted to or stored on any server controlled by Arcknowledge.

2. Cookies & Local Browser Storage

We do not set advertising cookies, cross-site tracking pixels, or behavioral profiling identifiers. Your browser may store lightweight, strictly functional UI preferences (such as expanded category states) in local session memory during your visit. You may clear your browser cache or storage at any time without affecting access to the directory.

3. Third-Party External Websites

Arcknowledge provides direct outbound hyperlinks to hundreds of independent third-party platforms worldwide. Once you click an external link and leave Arcknowledge, this Privacy Policy no longer applies. Each third-party service maintains its own privacy practices, cookie policies, and data processing standards, which we strongly encourage you to review before submitting personal information.

4. Children's Privacy

Because Arcknowledge does not collect, solicit, or store personal data from any visitor, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in applicable jurisdictions). Parents and guardians are encouraged to supervise minors when navigating outbound links to general-audience third-party platforms.

5. International Privacy Rights (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA & POPIA)

We respect global data protection frameworks, including the European Union & UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Because we process zero personal data on our servers, there is no personal record held for access, rectification, portability, or deletion requests—your privacy is protected by architectural default.

6. Security, Policy Updates & Contact

All outbound links curated in Arcknowledge utilize secure HTTPS protocols where supported by the destination platform. We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect improvements to the directory. For privacy inquiries or directory corrections, contact the editorial maintainer at privacy@arcknowledge.org.
